## Line 242 (summary)
It was uncertain whether point-cloud grasp scoring could select stable grasps on mixed bins when more than a quarter of each part was hidden, because published grasp-quality metrics assume full surface visibility.

## Line 244 (summary)
The team built an occlusion test harness and compared full-surface scoring with visibility-weighted sampling across 220 trials on opaque parts at 10, 20 and 30% occlusion.

## Line 246 (summary)
The team determined that:
1. Visibility-weighted sampling held above 80% grasp success up to 30% occlusion on opaque parts, while full-surface scoring fell below 60% at 20%.
2. Depth returns from thin and transparent parts were too sparse for point-cloud grasp scoring beyond 30% occlusion; those classes were left unresolved.
